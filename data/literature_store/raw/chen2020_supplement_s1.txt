S4 3D reconstruction of the electrodes 
 
The 2D images of the electrodes were aligned and cropped accordingly: 62 
  55 
  57 µm3 for 
the positive electrode and 47 
  49 
  20 µm3 for the negative electrode, in order to remove any 
unwanted areas of the morphologies of the electrodes and reconstructed with the use of 
commercial software Synopsys Simpleware Scan IP [1]. A median filter was applied in order 
to remove noise. 
The segmentation was achieved using a binary thresholding in order to reconstruct the 3D pore 
network. Then, the copy of pore domain was created and inverted on all slic es in the whole 
cuboid domain by employing Boolean operation to create a new mask of solid constituents. 
The volume fractions of the electrode components: active material and non-active, comprising 
of carbon black and pores were calculated by dividing the number of voxel assigned to one 
phase by the total number of voxels.
